Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy describes how SocialVerified actually handles data based on how the app is built today. It is provided as a good-faith, plain-language description and is not a substitute for legal advice. If you are relying on this document for legal compliance in your jurisdiction, have it reviewed by a qualified professional.

1. Who we are

SocialVerified is a social media platform operated by SocialVerified. You can reach us at socialverifiedofficial@gmail.com with any privacy questions or requests.

2. Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, username, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password (we never store your actual password in readable form).

Depending on how you use the app, we may also collect:

  • Profile information you choose to add: bio, profile photo, cover photo, location, website, gender, and birthday
  • Content you post: text posts, photos, videos, stories, reels, comments, and reactions
  • Information about your activity: who you follow, who follows you, likes, saves, searches, and posts you view
  • Technical information such as your general online/offline status, used to power features like "active now" indicators

3. Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted

Your direct messages are encrypted on your device before they are sent, using keys that never leave your device. This means we do not have the technical ability to read the contents of your private messages. We can see who you message and when, since that metadata is needed to deliver messages, but not what you actually said.

4. How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account and let you use the app's features
  • To show you a feed, suggest people to follow, and display trending content
  • To send you account-related emails, such as email verification codes and password reset codes
  • To respond to reports of content or accounts that violate our Terms of Use
  • To keep the platform secure and prevent abuse

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Who we share information with

We use a small number of service providers to run SocialVerified, each of whom only receives the data necessary to perform their function:

  • Cloudinary - stores and delivers photos and videos you upload
  • Supabase - hosts our database (your account information, posts, and encrypted message data)
  • Render - hosts the application server
  • Google (Gmail) - sends account verification and password reset emails on our behalf

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit or delete most of your profile information at any time from your account settings
  • You can set your account to private, controlling who can follow you and see your posts
  • You can control whether your birthday and gender are visible publicly, to followers only, or only to you
  • You can turn off "active status" so others cannot see when you're online
  • You can delete your account permanently at any time from Settings, which removes your profile, posts, and messages from our systems

7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. Stories are automatically deleted 24 hours after posting. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed from our active systems; some information may persist briefly in backups before being purged.

8. Children's privacy

SocialVerified is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. Depending on where you live, local law may set a higher age for using social platforms without a parent or guardian's involvement - please make sure you meet the age requirement that applies to you before signing up.

9. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data, including password hashing, encrypted messaging, and secure connections (HTTPS) between your device and our servers. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the app changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

11. Contact us

Questions, concerns, or requests about your data can be sent to socialverifiedofficial@gmail.com.
